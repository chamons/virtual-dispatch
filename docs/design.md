A roguelike hacking simulator set it the future inspired by Uplink, CyberKnight Flashpoint, and Hacknet.

# Theme

In the near future much has changed: the balkinization of the internet, rise quantum computing, colonization of the moon, and corporate takeover the government and public sphere.

Born and raised on what remains of the net, you have been able to scratch and save to setup a rig with enough horse power to compete with low corp systems.


# Player Factions

- Corporate
- Criminal
- Academic
- Open Source Hackers
- Government
- Offworld Independence

# Gameplay

There are two inter joined systems:

- A hacking minigame where you have to run through systems before you are caught
- A life simulator where you spent time improving your skills, buying and crafting gear, and surviving in a tough world

Over time the difficult increases until a crisis will provide a final test.


# Hacking

Starting a run spends focus and gives you some period of time before you are forced to disconnect.

Loading and using programs taking time.
